About Keyvany

  • About Keyvany
  • Inside Keyvany

In 2018, Keyvany GmbH was founded with the aim of taking luxury cars to a new level of refinement.

With over 20 years of experience in the exclusive automotive sector, the processing of carbon fibre composites, as well as leather processing, we have a team of outstanding employees in every department of the company.

We are specialized in the production of high quality carbon components. From the first design, through production, to final assembly, everything remains under the control of Keyvany.

We were already able to set a clear signal in 2019. With the Lamborghini Urus, the Bentley Continental GT and the Mercedes G-Class we presented the first three vehicles "Made by Keyvany".

Some more projects will not be long in coming and are planned for 2020. A Porsche 992 will be released by Keyvany at the beginning of 2020 and will also be presented at the Geneva Motor Show, like the models mentioned above.

Convince yourself of the quality of our products. Benefit from committed employees who contribute their many years of know-how in a professional working environment. With modern innovative technology, our specialists create individual luxury, perfect beauty and uniqueness that will inspire you. From the individual unique piece to the standardized product, the quality is in the foreground and finally a satisfied smiling customer.

Immerse yourself in the fascinating world of Keyvany and make your dream come true. No wish is too far-fetched, no vision too unrealistic and no idea too crazy.

Your dream becomes reality with us. Let our engineers and designers advise you. Whether luxurious or sporty, whether car or yacht - we are your competent partner.
